Why Innovation Academy?

Closing the chasm between Theory and Trade.

Sri Lanka is home to remarkable technical talent and academic excellence. Yet, countless brilliant ideas and research prototypes never reach commercial readiness due to the lack of product engineering rigor, market alignment, and venture architecture.

Established at TRACE Expert City, the nucleus of Sri Lanka's technology and knowledge ecosystem, the Innovation Academy provides the mentorship, tooling, lab environments, and industry connections necessary to take your invention to national and international markets.

Validated Product Ideation

Move beyond assumptions. Apply real design sprint frameworks, customer development interviews, and rigorous technical feasibility studies.

Engineering Rigor & AI Integration

Architect production-grade backbones leveraging modern cloud architectures, artificial intelligence, edge IoT, and automated pipelines.

Intellectual Property & Commercialization

Learn how to protect your patents and trade secrets, structure viable business units, and present convincingly to global investors.

Program Roadmap

The 16-Week Acceleration Journey

A step-by-step phased approach from problem validation to commercial presentation.

Phase 1 • Weeks 1–4

Discovery & Problem Validation

Identify unmet market needs, define core value propositions, conduct user discovery sprints, and benchmark competitive landscapes.

Phase 2 • Weeks 5–8

Technical Architecture & R&D

Evaluate hardware/software trade-offs, spin up development sandboxes, build initial proof-of-concept spikes, and integrate core AI/data layers.

Phase 3 • Weeks 9–12

MVP Build & User Testing

Assemble interactive prototypes, conduct real-user stress testing, iterate based on empirical feedback, and refine operational reliability.

Phase 4 • Weeks 13–16

Go-To-Market & Demo Day

Finalize product dossiers, IP protection blueprints, business proposals, and pitch live to institutional investors, venture partners, and corporate leaders.

Target Profile

Who Belongs in the Academy

Designed for individuals and early-stage teams committed to building high-impact technology solutions.

Emerging Technologists & Engineers

Graduates and early-career software, electronics, or biomedical engineers eager to move beyond ordinary code and create transformative intellectual property.

Aspiring Tech Founders

Individuals with high-conviction concepts looking for the technical rigor, mentorship network, and validation needed to launch an investable venture.

R&D Researchers & Inventors

Academics, postgrads, or hobbyist innovators with working research papers or prototypes ready to be transformed into commercial-grade products.

Corporate Intrapreneurs

Enterprise innovators seeking to build internal spin-offs, disruptive workflows, and agile new business units within established firms.
